Before acting on this list

  • Data is from today's collection. Volumes added or removed since the last VSP 360 collection run won't show yet.
  • No LUNs doesn't mean unused. WWN registration wasn't checked. A host group with WWNs and no LUNs may be waiting for provisioning.
  • Replication and DR groups may be empty by design. Groups tagged replication / DR (GAD, HUR, DGR) often get volumes only during a failover or test.
  • Default groups are listed separately. Every port has a default host group 0 (xx-G00). The 51 empty ones are collapsed under each array.
  • Fibre Channel host groups only. iSCSI targets may be stored under a separate resource type and aren't included.
